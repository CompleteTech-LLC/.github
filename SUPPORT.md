# Support

## Getting help

- **Usage questions and bugs:** open an issue on the relevant repository. Include what you ran, what you expected, and what happened.
- **Security issues:** do not open an issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else (collaboration, business inquiries):** email Timothy.Gregg@complete.tech or find us at [complete.tech](https://complete.tech) and [@CompleteTechLLC](https://x.com/CompleteTechLLC) on X.

## Expectations

This is a small team. Projects are published as-is, many are prototypes, and we cannot guarantee response times or support for any specific repository.
