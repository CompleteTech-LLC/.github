# Code of Conduct

Complete Tech LLC wants its public repositories to be places where people can collaborate respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith and focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam or deliberate disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces for the organization's repositories.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and restrict participation that violates this code. Report concerns to Timothy.Gregg@complete.tech; reports are handled privately.
